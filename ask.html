Ask The Cosmos

Found a claim we haven't covered?

Drop a conspiracy, theory, or argument below. If it attempts to rewrite physics or astronomy, we'll break down the math, the science, and a DIY experiment to test it — and publish the answer here for everyone.

Submit for Analysis

Runs on Web3Forms — free, no backend required. To go live, grab an access key at web3forms.com and drop it into the access_key field in ask.html. Submissions land in your inbox; answered ones get added to the backlog below by editing data/debunks.json.

How this works

  1. You submit a claim, in your own words, with as much detail as you have.
  2. We research it against primary evidence — physics, published data, or a reproducible experiment.
  3. The answer is written up plainly, with sources and a DIY test where one exists.
  4. It's added to the public backlog below, building an open, crowd-sourced library of refutations.
