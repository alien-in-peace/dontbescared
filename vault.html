01

The Shape & Motion of Earth

The Myth

"The Earth is flat, enclosed under a firmament / dome."

The Observed Reality
  • Horizon dip increases predictably with altitude and matches spherical-Earth geometry, measurable with a phone inclinometer.
  • The Coriolis force deflects long-range artillery, hurricanes, and ocean currents in a direction that only makes sense on a rotating sphere.
  • A Foucault pendulum precesses over the course of a day — visible proof the ground beneath it is turning.
  • Ships and distant coastlines disappear hull-first over the horizon, and reappear top-first through a zoom lens exactly as curvature predicts.

02

Spaceflight & Satellites

The Myth

"Space is fake — satellites don't exist, orbiting is a hoax."

The Observed Reality
  • The ISS is visible to the naked eye and its transits can be predicted to the second from public orbital elements — then verified by simply looking up.
  • GPS satellites require relativistic time-dilation corrections for their orbital speed and altitude; without that correction, GPS would drift by kilometres per day.
  • Amateur radio operators worldwide bounce signals off real satellites (and off the Moon) using equipment anyone can buy.
  • Thousands of independent amateur astronomers track and photograph the ISS, Starlink trains, and other satellites nightly with consumer telescopes.

03

Gravity & Forces

The Myth

"There's no gravity — only buoyancy and density."

The Observed Reality
  • The Cavendish experiment measures two lead spheres attracting each other inside a sealed vacuum chamber — no air, no buoyancy possible, only mass attracting mass.
  • In a vacuum chamber, a feather and a hammer fall at the identical rate — buoyancy and density would separate them, gravity does not.
  • Dense submarines still float and rise on command, directly contradicting "denser objects always sink" as a universal rule.

04

Deep Space & Optics

The Myth

"Deep space images are CGI or a painted dome."

The Observed Reality
  • Amateur astronomers reproduce Hubble- and JWST-class images from their own backyards, stacking their own RAW sensor data — no studio required.
  • Spectral redshift is independently reproducible with a sub-$100 spectroscope pointed at a bright star.
  • Competing space agencies (NASA, Roscosmos, ESA, CNSA, ISRO, JAXA) would need decades of impossible secret coordination to fake consistent, cross-checkable data.
